Mouse and touch

Press and hold the buttons as on the pedal. Drag a knob or use the wheel on it. Click PARA for PUSH. With a knob in focus the arrow keys turn that knob.

The tuner is a long press of HOME: OpenPocket does nothing special with both footswitches, whatever the panel says.
